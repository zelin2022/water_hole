# Water Hole development

- Work on `main` and push completed, tested changes to `origin/main` by default.
- The current milestone is the provisional demo in `docs/DEMO.md`, with visual direction in `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md`. `docs/V0.md` records the completed prototype.
- Use test-driven development: write a meaningful failing test, run it and confirm the expected failure, implement the smallest working change, then refactor with tests passing.
- Keep terrain, liquid simulation, movement, and economy independent of rendering and Electron so they can be tested deterministically.
- Water must remain accounted for across the world, tank, and machine. Selling must not duplicate credits.
- Tank capacity has nine purchases beyond base, giving ten levels from 20 to 200 water in increments of 20. Successive provisional prices are 10/15/20/25/35/45/60/80/110 credits (400 total). Collection stays at 36 water per second. At upgrade level L (0–9), a full unload takes `0.8 * (1 + L / 9)` seconds and transfer rate is capacity divided by that duration: 25 water per second at base and 125 at maximum. A full 200-water tank takes 1.6 seconds, twice the base 20-water tank's 0.8 seconds. Credit only actual transfer, respecting interruption; cumulative delivery no longer ends active runs or caps a transfer. Preserve older capacity levels 0/1/2 as 20/40/60 and retain actual water and credits without refunds; no new save fields are needed. Keep the visible backpack bounded at its previous 60-water size.
- The character auto-attaches on side contact only while falling. Detachment and wall jumps must not immediately reattach to the same wall. Digging while attached requires the purchased work harness.
- Two-cell walking steps are the selected movement behaviour. Remove the development comparison and normalize earlier one-cell saved runs to two cells without changing their world.
- Demo mining has seven radius purchases at 15/20/30/45/65/90/125 credits (390 total). Damage and speed each have three purchases at 30/55/95 credits (180 per track). Natural durability is sand 1, earth 3, stone 6, basalt 10, and granite 16. Sand occurs sparsely within sixteen metres of the local surface; harder deposits become more common deeper down. Keep damage tiers 1/2/3/6, discrete impacts and scaled wind-up animation, and protect the reinforced foundation and sealed map boundary at maximum power. All mining purchases cost 750 credits; adding the first tank purchase and work harness costs 840. Prices remain provisional for the 2000-metre descent; no water-delivery cap prevents buying the complete set. Migrate older saves with no digging-speed entry to level zero. Show partial terrain damage in four stages based on the fraction of material HP lost, from bruising and cracks to chipped, nearly broken faces. Clip marks to their solid cell and apply normal scene lighting; rendering must not alter collision, damage, or water.
- New sites use 384 by 2053 four-pixel cells, allowing 2000 metres of standing-player HUD depth. Extend water placement through the deeper site while preserving the size formula and independent dry rolls. Save version 2 uses lossless compact grids; read version 1 saves without resizing or regenerating their worlds. Older runs need a new expedition to obtain the deeper map.
- Jump power and wall-climbing speed each have seven purchases beyond base at 15/20/30/45/65/90/125 credits, costing 390 per track. Double jump costs 100 credits and recharges only on ground or wall contact. Climbing speeds are 75/135/200/280/350/440/550/680 world pixels per second. Base ground/wall launches are approximately 159/120 with approximately 300 upward gravity, preserving starter jump height; upgraded jumps and all falling use gravity 900. Keep the wall-lip assist height unchanged when tuning upward gravity. Wall-origin jumps and their air jump use a launch speed capped at 1.6 times the climbing speed for the jump equipment's tier; optimized repeated wall/air-jump ascent must stay roughly equal to or below same-tier climbing. Preserve air-jump availability and wall-origin chain state in saves; missing older entries do not grant equipment or a fresh airborne jump. Mining plus both mobility tracks and double jump cost 1,630 credits; all purchases remain optional before reaching the bottom.
- The developer panel includes teleporting to a clear location near the actual saved hopper and warping to the demo ending for survey testing. Teleport preserves resources and terrain, resets motion and pending actions, and rejects completed runs or blocked destinations. Ending warp relocates to a clear bottom position, resets pending movement/actions, and completes the demo without consuming water or granting credits; it works on dry worlds and preserves valid saves. Release builds must remove all development tools.
- Preserve the original quiet synthesized audio style while continuously blending music by current depth and tank fill. The depth blend spans 0 to 1600 HUD metres. Smooth scene and volume changes without restarting phrases; music and effects settings remain independent.
- New Game has no water-pocket or full-visibility choices: start every new expedition with depth-weighted three-size pockets and normal dark-cave vision. Keep full visibility in the gated developer panel. Preserve existing saved worlds and their layout metadata. Dark caves with temporary sonar outlines are the selected vision mode; remove permanent remembered outlines and migrate older vision choices. The headlamp and visible head follow mouse aim. Sonar uses tunable two-second travel and half-second per-outline appearance/fade.
- Torso-lamp radius, headlamp width, and headlamp depth each have two purchases, multiplying the previous value by 1.2 per level (44% above base when complete). Base torso radius stays 55 world pixels; base headlamp range is 126 world pixels (30% below the earlier 180), and its full cone angle is 64.8 degrees (10% below the earlier 72). Within this small halo, reveal solid terrain and its damage without line of sight; never reveal water or sealed cave interiors. Moving away hides it again. Derive the halo from existing equipment without new save fields; development omni vision remains independent.

- Default water generation uses three size classes with nominal volumes 45/90/180 and uniform integer variation ±15/30/60 (ranges 30–60/60–120/120–240). At HUD depth d, small/medium and medium/large frequency ratios both equal r = 3 * 0.9^((d - 50) / 100), giving weights r²:r:1. Sample class and volume independently. All classes are possible at every depth; the starter is always small with a variable capacity and a guaranteed accessible edge. Every pocket, including the starter and legacy-layout pockets, independently has a 1-in-5 chance to start dry; use a separate seeded stream without changing size probabilities or forcing a wet quota. Separate hollow caves are post-demo work. Retain older layouts internally for tests and save compatibility, and never regenerate saved worlds.

Preserve actual terrain, entrance, and machine placement when loading older saves.

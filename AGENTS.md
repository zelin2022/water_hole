# Water Hole development

- Work on `main` and push completed, tested changes to `origin/main` by default.
- The current milestone is the provisional demo in `docs/DEMO.md`, with visual direction in `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md`. `docs/V0.md` records the completed prototype.
- Use test-driven development: write a meaningful failing test, run it and confirm the expected failure, implement the smallest working change, then refactor with tests passing.
- Keep terrain, liquid simulation, movement, and economy independent of rendering and Electron so they can be tested deterministically.
- Water must remain accounted for across the world, tank, and machine. Selling must not duplicate credits.
- The character auto-attaches on side contact only while falling. Detachment and wall jumps must not immediately reattach to the same wall. Digging while attached requires the purchased work harness.
- Two-cell walking steps are the selected movement behaviour. Remove the development comparison and normalize earlier one-cell saved runs to two cells without changing their world.
- Demo mining showcases seven radius purchases, three damage purchases, and three speed purchases beyond base equipment. Earth durability is 1 and stone is 6, making damage tiers 1/2/3/6 require 6/3/2/1 stone hits. Keep discrete impacts and scaled wind-up animation, protect the reinforced foundation at maximum power, and price the showcase so it is attainable before the delivery cutoff. Migrate older saves with no digging-speed entry to level zero.
- The developer panel includes teleporting to a clear location near the actual saved hopper. Preserve resources and terrain, reset motion and pending actions, and reject completed runs or blocked destinations. Release builds must remove all development tools.
- Preserve the original quiet synthesized audio style while continuously blending music by current depth and tank fill. Smooth scene and volume changes without restarting phrases; music and effects settings remain independent.
- Keep water-pocket distributions selectable before a run and preserve that choice in saves. Dark caves with temporary sonar outlines are the selected vision mode; remove permanent remembered outlines and migrate older vision choices. The headlamp and visible head follow mouse aim. Sonar uses tunable two-second travel and half-second per-outline appearance/fade.
- Default water generation uses three size classes with nominal volumes 45/90/180 and uniform integer variation ±15/30/60 (ranges 30–60/60–120/120–240). At HUD depth d, small/medium and medium/large frequency ratios both equal r = 3 * 0.9^((d - 50) / 100), giving weights r²:r:1. Sample class and volume independently. All classes are possible at every depth; the starter is always small with a variable capacity and a guaranteed accessible edge. Every pocket, including the starter and legacy-layout pockets, independently has a 1-in-5 chance to start dry; use a separate seeded stream without changing size probabilities or forcing a wet quota. Separate hollow caves are post-demo work. Preserve older layouts as labeled legacy comparison options and never regenerate saved worlds.
- New sites put the exchange on an indestructible reinforced platform against the left map boundary. Its foundation reaches the map bottom, with a flush climbable right face and no overhang. Keep all diggable terrain to its right. The starter shaft begins 480 world pixels beyond the platform edge (roughly two-thirds of a standard 1280-pixel window), with a flat walkable approach and its accessible starter pocket alongside. Preserve actual terrain, entrance, and machine placement when loading older saves.
- Cave sonar travels through connected open space and traces only its adjacent wall faces. Terrain blocks it, including diagonal corner gaps; sealed water pockets stay hidden. The original penetrating scan is retained as the hidden Subsurface scanner.
- The demo ends at its cumulative delivery limit with a thank-you screen; do not present the limit as a mission or continue simulation after it. Artifact searching remains outside the demo.
- The generated reference image defines visual style only. Its depicted content does not define gameplay, infrastructure, or HUD requirements.
- Before finishing a change, run `npm test` and `npm run build`. Playtest changes to controls or rendering in the browser or Electron.
